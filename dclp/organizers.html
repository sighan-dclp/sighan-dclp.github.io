Review

Review process

Two submission routes

Direct submission through OpenReview, plus papers reviewed through ACL Rolling Review, subject to the ARR deadlines for the host conference.

Three reviews per paper

Every submission receives three reviews.

Bounded reviewer load

We anticipate 25–35 submissions. The committee is sized to keep reviewer load at no more than three papers per reviewer under normal conditions.
