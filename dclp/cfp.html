Call for Papers

SIGHAN-DCLP 2027 invites work on Chinese language processing that moves beyond contemporary, general-domain Standard Mandarin — across linguistic varieties, specialized domains, historical periods, and geographic communities.

Topics of interest

We welcome long papers, short papers, and shared-task system descriptions on any of the following, as well as broader Chinese NLP work — with particular emphasis on reusable resources and benchmarks and on systematic evaluation of current LLMs.

Linguistic diversity

  • Sinitic varieties: Cantonese/Yue, Wu, Min, Hakka, Gan, Xiang
  • Low-resource Sino-Tibetan languages
  • Resource creation and annotation for under-served varieties
  • Modeling, cross-variety transfer, and multilingual/multi-varietal representation
  • Speech and text processing for spoken varieties
  • LLM adaptation to varieties with little written data

Domain diversity

  • Specialized Chinese in law, traditional Chinese medicine, science, and finance
  • Domain adaptation and domain-specific pretraining
  • Information extraction from specialized text
  • Retrieval-augmented methods and knowledge-intensive reasoning
  • Reliability, factuality, and failure analysis of generative AI in specialized domains

Temporal diversity

  • Classical and historical Chinese: segmentation, syntactic and semantic analysis
  • Historical language modeling and diachronic change
  • Information extraction from historical corpora
  • Alignment and translation between historical and modern Chinese

Geographic and sociolinguistic diversity

  • Chinese across mainland China, Taiwan, Hong Kong, Macao, Singapore, Malaysia, and other communities
  • Regional variation in vocabulary, orthography, and grammar
  • Code-switching and contact phenomena
  • Evaluation sets that make regional variation measurable

Cross-cutting

  • Reusable resources, benchmarks, and evaluation methodology for Chinese NLP
  • Systematic evaluation of current LLMs beyond contemporary general-domain Mandarin
  • Documentation of representational limitations and potential biases
  • Chinese semantic parsing and meaning representation — see the shared task

Submission

The workshop uses OpenReview for direct submissions and also accepts papers reviewed through ACL Rolling Review (ARR), subject to the ARR deadlines for the host conference.

  • Direct submission — through OpenReview; link to be announced.
  • ARR commitment — commit a reviewed ARR submission to the workshop; deadline to be announced.

Formatting follows the standard ACL style files for the host conference. Page limits, anonymity requirements, and the camera-ready schedule will be posted with the final call.

Questions about the call are welcome at sighandclp@gmail.com.

Ethics

Authors are expected to follow the ACL Code of Ethics. We ask authors releasing data to state licensing clearly, document provenance, and protect private or sensitive information. Work on regional, historical, and community varieties should avoid deficit-based characterizations and should document representational limitations and potential biases.

Review process

Every submission receives three reviews. We anticipate 25–35 submissions, and the programme committee is sized so that reviewer load stays at no more than three papers per reviewer under normal conditions.

The programme committee is listed on the organizers page.

Venue and format

The workshop is a one-day event with invited talks, oral presentations, posters, a shared-task session, and a panel or moderated discussion, run in hybrid form so that remote presenters take part in the same sessions as in-person presenters. The venue will be announced here.
