Workshop archive

Ten numbered SIGHAN workshops and three CIPS–SIGHAN joint conferences have been held since 2002, together with a Chinese Language Processing workshop in 2000 that predates the SIG. Proceedings for every edition are permanently archived in the ACL Anthology; where an edition's own website is still online, it is linked here too.
