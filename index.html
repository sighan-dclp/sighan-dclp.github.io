Association for Computational Linguistics

Special Interest Group on Chinese Language Processing

SIGHAN provides an umbrella for researchers in academia and industry working on every aspect of Chinese language processing. Since 2001 the group has sponsored ten numbered workshops, several CIPS–SIGHAN joint conferences, and the shared-task evaluations — the SIGHAN bakeoffs — that set long-running benchmarks for the field.
